# Alloy4Fun

The Alloy4Fun is a web platform to support the teaching of the Alloy formal method, allowing:
- the sharing of Alloy models, specifications and instances with embedded themes via permalinks
- the creation of specification challenges in which students try to guess a secret specification
- the collection of models and specifications to build a valuable corpus of data to support research

Alloy4Fun is built on top of [Alloy 6](https://alloytools.org/) and is currently deployed at [alloy4fun.inesctec.pt](http://alloy4fun.inesctec.pt/).
